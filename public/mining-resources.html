Mining &
Natural Resources

Disciplined geological exploration, open-pit quarry operations, mineral aggregate production, and responsible environmental stewardship.

Operating Mandate

Foundations from first principles.

Industrial infrastructure and modern cities cannot stand without rock, gravel, sand, and essential minerals. In an era of rapid regional urbanization, accessing certified aggregate deposits is the single most critical dependency for transport corridors, maritime ports, and capital construction.

ON & E approaches mining with long-term institutional permanence. Rather than rapid, destructive extraction, we design integrated quarry concessions that balance continuous crushing throughput with acoustic dampening, dust containment, water recycling, and progressive pit rehabilitation.

01 · Geological Evaluation

Resource Mapping

Subsurface geophysical profiling, core drilling analysis, and 3D deposit modeling. We verify rock compressive strength, abrasive indices, and yield density before breaking ground.

02 · Extraction & Blasting

Precision Operations

Controlled bench blasting with electronic detonation delays, optimized powder factors, vibration monitoring, and high-safety burden control to minimize community impact.

03 · Crushing & Processing

Material Sizing

Multi-stage jaw, cone, and vertical shaft impact (VSI) crushing circuits producing precision aggregates, manufactured sand, ballast, and base-course rock to strict BS/ASTM specifications.

Environmental Responsibility

Stewardship built into every phase.

Mining operations must account for the land before, during, and after extraction. Our operations feature closed-circuit wash water recycling, zero-effluent sedimentation ponds, and systematic topsoil conservation.
