Capability 04 · Power & Transition

Energy &
Infrastructure

Decentralized power generation, commercial microgrids, hybrid solar-storage architectures, and resilient industrial utilities.

Operating Mandate

Uninterrupted power for heavy industry.

Industrial facilities cannot operate on unstable grids. A single voltage dip can trip automated motor control centers, stall crushing circuits, and cause hundreds of thousands of dollars in mechanical downtime and clearing delays.

ON & E engineers high-availability power infrastructure tailored to heavy industry. We design and install hybrid microgrids that combine solar photovoltaic arrays, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and intelligent synchronous generator controls—guaranteeing 99.98% power uptime while cutting diesel reliance and emissions.

01 · Hybrid Microgrids

Decentralized Generation

Autonomous power stations engineered to balance solar PV, diesel/gas generation, and high-discharge battery banks for off-grid industrial plants and processing hubs.

02 · Battery Storage (BESS)

Peak Shaving & Power Quality

Containerized lithium iron phosphate (LFP) storage providing instantaneous frequency regulation, black-start capacity, and reactive power compensation for heavy inductive motor loads.

03 · Energy Transition Strategy

Decarbonization Pathways

Phased transition models that replace pure thermal diesel generation with clean baseline solar, lowering kilowatt-hour costs and reducing long-term environmental footprints.
